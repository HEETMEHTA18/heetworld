# ML Experiments

Alongside larger projects, I run smaller machine-learning experiments to strengthen my understanding of the fundamentals.

## What's in the lab

- Supervised learning and Scikit-learn workflows.
- TensorFlow experiments and training loops.
- Dataset preprocessing and model evaluation.
- Image processing and computer-vision experiments.
- NLP experiments and LLM integration.

## The point

My goal with these projects is to understand not just how to use a framework, but **how the underlying ML pipeline works** — from the dataset and the math through to evaluation and deployment.
