# Sasta Shark Tank

An AI-powered application inspired by startup pitching and investment evaluation.

## The concept

The app explores how AI can assist in:

- Evaluating startup ideas.
- Generating investor-style feedback.
- Asking questions about a business.
- Simulating a Shark Tank-style experience.
- Helping students and early founders improve their ideas.

## What I explored

- **LLM integration** for structured evaluation.
- **Prompt engineering** to make feedback specific rather than generic.
- **Web application development** for an interactive workflow.
- **Interactive user flows** where the model pushes back on weak assumptions.

## Why it matters

Practicing the pitch is half the battle. A system that asks the awkward questions a real investor would — before you're in the room — is genuinely useful for early-stage founders.
