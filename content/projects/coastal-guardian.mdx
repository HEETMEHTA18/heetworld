# Coastal Guardian

Deep Learning for Coastal Threat Classification using satellite imagery and transfer learning.

## Approach

- Designed a CNN on NASA satellite imagery to classify 4 coastal threat categories (erosion, pollution, algal bloom, clear)
- Used ResNet50 transfer learning and augmentation on imbalanced classes
- Achieved 87%+ validation accuracy on 2,400 image patches
- Evaluated with confusion matrix, ROC-AUC and precision-recall
